A few helpful answers

Before we get started.

Can you help with an existing solar system?

Yes. We offer fault diagnosis, repairs, maintenance and compliance upgrades for existing solar systems. Tell us about your equipment and the problem you’re experiencing.

Do you work on homes and businesses?

Yes. Our solar and electrical services cover residential and commercial properties. Contact us to discuss the scope and location of your project.

What should I include in a quote request?

Tell us your location, the service you need and a little about your current setup. For an existing solar system, the inverter and battery models can help us understand what you have.

Do you issue electrical compliance certificates?

Yes. Get in touch about your installation so we can discuss the requirements and any work that may be needed.
